Add `record()` and `Recording`, which record warnings (`warnings.showwarning`), unraisable exceptions (`sys.unraisablehook`) and exceptions that end a thread (`threading.excepthook`) while active, as `WarningRecord`, `UnraisableRecord` and `ThreadExceptionRecord`. Warning filters in the `-W` syntax apply for the recording's lifetime, `take()` splits a recording, and a `GCPolicy` says when garbage is collected so finalizer errors land in the recording that made the garbage. A recorder called after its recording ended emits `RecorderExpiredWarning` and passes the event on. See `docs/design/warnings.md`.
